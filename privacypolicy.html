Privacy
Policy

At IT Study Hub, we value your privacy as much as your education. This document outlines how we collect, protect, and handle your data across our platforms.

Section 01

Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, purchase a premium module, or contact support. The types of information we may collect include:
  • Name and contact information (Email address).
  • Academic progress and course completion data.
  • Payment metadata (handled via secure encrypted gateways).
  • Technical log data (IP address, browser type).
Section 02

How We Use Data

Your data is primarily used to provide a seamless learning experience. We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers. We use your data to:
  • Track your progress across C, Java, and Python modules.
  • Send critical platform updates and security alerts.
  • Personalize your dashboard and "Study Materials" recommendations.
Section 03

Cookies & Tracking

We use local storage and cookies to keep you signed in and to remember your progress (like completed modules and quiz passes). These are essential for the platform to work. When you first visit, a notice lets you choose whether we may also run privacy-friendly analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and is not used for advertising). You can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of the homepage. You can also disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of IT Study Hub may stop working.
Section 04

Third-Party Services

We rely on a few trusted services to run the site: Google Firebase (sign-in and account data), Cloudflare (hosting, security and optional analytics), Razorpay (payments; we never see or store your card details) and an email provider that delivers sign-in codes. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.
Section 05

Your Rights & Contact

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and its data, and you can withdraw your consent for optional analytics at any time. We keep your data only as long as your account exists or as needed for payment records. Email admin@itstudyhub.dpdns.org for any of these requests or for a privacy complaint. For refunds, see our Refund & Cancellation Policy. IT Study Hub is not directed at children under 13.
