Privacy
Policy

At IT Study Hub, we value your privacy as much as your education. This document outlines how we collect, protect, and handle your data across our platforms.

Section 01

Information Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, purchase a premium module, or contact support. The types of information we may collect include:
  • Name and contact information (Email address).
  • Academic progress and course completion data.
  • Payment metadata (handled via secure encrypted gateways).
  • Technical log data (IP address, browser type).
Section 02

How We Use Data

Your data is primarily used to provide a seamless learning experience. We do not sell your personal data to third-party advertisers. We use your data to:
  • Track your progress across C, Java, and Python modules.
  • Provide certificates of completion.
  • Send critical platform updates and security alerts.
  • Personalize your dashboard and "Study Materials" recommendations.
Section 03

Cookies & Tracking

We use local storage and cookies to remember your progress (like the "Mark as Complete" status) and keep you logged in. These are essential for the platform's core functionality. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features of the IT Study Hub may stop working.
Section 04

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics (like Google Analytics) to understand platform traffic and secure payment processors for premium upgrades. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.
