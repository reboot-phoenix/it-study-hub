Frequently Asked Questions
How do I create an account? ▼
Head over to the Login page and click "Sign up free". Enter your name, email, and a password. That's it — your account is created instantly and your quiz scores start saving automatically.
Why is my quiz score not saving? ▼
Quiz scores only save if you're signed in before taking the quiz. If you completed a quiz while logged out, the score won't be retroactively saved. Sign in first, then retake the quiz — it'll save this time.
What's the difference between the course levels? ▼
Each course has three tiers — Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced. Beginner modules are free. Intermediate and Advanced are Premium. Not sure which level you're at? Take the Placement Quiz — it'll tell you exactly where to start.
How does Premium work? ▼
Premium unlocks all Intermediate and Advanced lessons, PDF notes, and cheat sheets. You can pick the Starter plan (1 course), Pro (all 6 courses, 12 months), or Elite (lifetime access + mentor Q&A). Payment is handled securely via Razorpay.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▼
Go to the Login page and click "Forgot password?". Enter your registered email and we'll send a reset link. Check your spam folder if you don't see it within a few minutes.
The login page isn't working / I'm stuck on a blank screen. ▼
Try a hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R on Windows, Cmd + Shift + R on Mac). If that doesn't work, clear your browser cache and cookies for this site, then try again. Still stuck? Use the contact form below and we'll sort it out.
How do I appear on the Leaderboard? ▼
Complete any placement quiz while signed in. Your score gets saved to Firestore and you'll automatically appear on the Global Leaderboard. Higher scores = higher rank.
