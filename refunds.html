Refund & Cancellation Policy

This page explains how refunds and cancellations work for IT Study Hub premium plans. Browsing the free modules never costs anything, and you can read the plan details on the pricing page before you pay.

1. What you are buying

Premium plans are one-time payments, not subscriptions. Nothing renews automatically and we never charge you again without a new payment from you. Starter gives access for 6 months, Pro for 12 months and Elite for lifetime access, as described on the pricing page.

2. 7-day refund window

If you are not satisfied, you can ask for a refund within 7 days of your purchase. Email admin@itstudyhub.dpdns.org from the email address on your account and include your Razorpay payment ID (it is on the payment receipt Razorpay emails you).

3. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through Razorpay. After we approve a refund, banks and card networks usually take a few working days to show it in your account. We do not control that part of the timeline.

4. When a refund may be declined

  • The request is made after the 7-day window.
  • The account has been used to share, resell or circumvent access to premium content, as described in our Terms of Service.

5. Cancelling

Because plans do not renew, there is nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends when your plan period ends (Elite does not expire). You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing the address above.

6. Failed or duplicate payments

If money was taken from your account but your plan did not activate, or you were charged twice, email us with the payment ID and we will fix it or refund the extra payment.
